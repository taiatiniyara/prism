PRISM AI

Transforming Pacific Utility Data into Clear, Actionable Decisions

What is PRISM AI? It's an intelligent assistant built directly into the PRISM benchmarking platform. Utility managers, engineers, financial analysts, and donor partners can ask questions in plain English — about performance, reliability, costs, or how they compare to peers — and get clear, data-backed answers in seconds. No spreadsheets, no manual report writing, no waiting.

1. Who Is This For?

Utility CEOs & General Managers

Get an instant snapshot of your utility's performance — where you're strong, where you're behind, and what needs attention. Walk into board meetings and donor discussions with confidence.

"How are we performing against our peers? What's our biggest risk right now?"

Operations & Engineering Teams

Track reliability metrics, system losses, generator efficiency, and workforce productivity. Spot problems before they become crises. Compare your network against similar utilities.

"Which feeders are causing the most outages? How efficient is our generation fleet?"

Finance & Planning Staff

Monitor cost recovery, tariff adequacy, and financial sustainability. Model the impact of tariff changes. Prepare donor-ready reports with real data in minutes instead of weeks.

"What happens if we raise residential tariffs by 5%? Are we covering our operating costs?"

Donors & Development Partners

Access verified utility performance data across the Pacific. Compare outcomes, identify where funding would have the greatest impact, and auto-fill grant applications with actual KPI data.

"Which utilities most urgently need renewable energy investment? Show me the data to support a GCF application."

3. How It Works

You ask a question in plain English. PRISM AI queries live utility data from Power BI dashboards and the PRISM database, applies advanced analytics, and returns a clear, contextual answer — usually within seconds. It checks multiple data sources automatically, uses the best available data, and is honest when data is missing.

It Understands Context

Tell it your utility and reporting year once. It remembers. Every subsequent question automatically scopes to your utility — no need to repeat yourself. Want to compare? Just ask "how do we compare to others?"

It Finds Hidden Patterns

Beyond answering direct questions, it actively looks for anomalies, correlations, and trends you might miss. It can tell you which utilities are most similar to yours, which KPIs move together, and where investment would have the greatest impact.

It Generates Reports Automatically

Need a quarterly board report? A donor application? An executive briefing? It pulls the latest data, formats it, flags concerns, and delivers a complete document in one request. No more manual compilation across spreadsheets.

It's Honest About Gaps

If data hasn't been submitted or is incomplete, it tells you — clearly and specifically. It won't invent numbers to appear helpful. This builds trust and helps you identify and close data gaps.

4. Real-World Use Cases

Scenario 1: Board Meeting Tomorrow
A utility CEO asks: "Give me a one-page briefing on our performance for the board." PRISM AI checks the latest data, pulls SAIDI, system losses, cost recovery, and electrification rates, flags a concerning uptick in diesel dependence, and delivers a board-ready summary with key numbers, trends, red flags, and recommendations — all in under 10 seconds.
Scenario 2: Donor Application Deadline
A planning officer needs to submit a Green Climate Fund proposal by Friday. They ask PRISM AI: "Auto-fill a GCF application for my utility using FY2023 data." The AI pulls real KPI values — diesel dependence percentage, renewable share, SAIDI, island vulnerability factors — and populates the narrative template with verified performance data. What used to take two weeks of data gathering now takes a single request.
Scenario 3: Identifying Investment Priorities
A regional development partner asks: "If we had $5M for reliability improvements, where should we invest?" PRISM AI ranks all Pacific utilities by where funding would have the most impact, factoring in SAIDI severity, customer numbers, diesel dependence, and financial vulnerability. It returns a prioritized list with recommended actions and estimated impact.
Scenario 4: Planning Renewable Transition
An engineer asks: "If we add 3MW of solar and 6MWh of battery storage, how much diesel would we displace?" PRISM AI models the scenario using actual capacity and generation data, projects diesel displacement percentage, estimates annual savings, and shows how your utility's diesel dependence compares to regional targets.

5. Key Design Principles

Conversational, Not Technical

You don't need to know database queries, DAX, or SQL. You ask questions the way you would ask a colleague. The AI handles all the technical complexity behind the scenes.

Data-Driven, Not Fabricated

Every number comes from actual utility data. If data isn't available, the AI says so — clearly and honestly. It will never invent numbers or present submission statistics as performance metrics.

Context-Aware

It understands Pacific utility realities: diesel dependence, island geography, small customer bases, donor reporting requirements, workforce challenges. Comparisons are fair — a 5-island utility with 5,000 customers isn't benchmarked against a mainland utility with 500,000.

Secure & Private

All data access is scoped to your utility and your role. Sensitive information is filtered. Every request is authenticated and validated. The AI never retains or shares your data outside the platform.

7. What Makes This Different?

  • Built for the Pacific, not adapted from elsewhere. Every feature reflects Pacific utility realities — small customer bases, multi-island service territories, diesel dependence, donor-driven funding, and workforce constraints.
  • Not just a chatbot. It's an analytics engine that surfaces problems before you ask, generates complete reports, models scenarios, and fills donor applications — all from the same interface.
  • Always transparent. If data is missing, you'll know. If a metric is based on estimates, it tells you. Trust is built through honesty, not through producing answers at any cost.
  • Designed for the people who use it. Utility CEOs, engineers, accountants, donor partners — everyone gets the same powerful capabilities through the same simple interface.
